This transaction did not complete normally. Tell the customer, in 2 to 3 short
sentences, what state it is in and what that means for their money.

Cover:
1. What was attempted and for how much.
2. Why it is in that state. The reason is given in the description - use it,
   and do not guess a different reason.
3. Whether the money has moved, and what happens next.

Use these rules about the states:
- Pending means the transaction has been accepted but has not settled yet, so
  the money has not moved.
- Failed means the transaction was rejected and no money moved.
- Cancelled means it was stopped before it settled and no money moved.

This is the style required:

  Your withdrawal of $5,000.00 from account 1003 was rejected because there
  was not enough money in the account. Nothing has been taken from your
  balance. You can try again for a smaller amount.

Now write that kind of message for the transaction below.
